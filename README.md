# Dewanganji-heritage-culture-language-
A digital archive of the authentic regional language and history of local Bengalis in the hilly and char areas of Dewanganj, Bakshiganj, Islampur, Fulchhari, Raumari, Rajibpur, and South West Garo Hills (Meghalaya). A unique blend of Kamrupi, Rangpuri (North Bengal), and partial Mymensingh languages and cultures.
